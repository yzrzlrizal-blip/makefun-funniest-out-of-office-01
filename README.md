The funniest out-of-office auto-reply in 100 characters: the auto-reply itself is also out of office, so please email someone who actually works here.

The entry line:

> Out of office. My auto-reply is also out of office, so please email someone who actually works here.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the reply to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), then re-reads it to verify the bytes. Pure standard library, deterministic: the same line on every run.
